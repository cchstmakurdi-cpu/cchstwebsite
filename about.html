About CCHST

Continental College of Health Sciences and Technology trains front-line health workers for Makurdi and Benue State — grounded in the realities of the health system they'll join, not just the theory of a textbook.

Who we are

A young college, building deliberately.

Founded in 2023 and based at Cornerstone Academy on Gboko Road, CCHST is still early in its life as an institution — and that shapes how it teaches.

Curriculum

Four focused programs

Community Health Extension Worker, Pharmacy Technician, Medical Laboratory Technician, and Public Health Technician — each a 3-year programme open to SSCE holders.

Location

Built in Makurdi, for Benue

CCHST trains students for the primary health centres, laboratories, and pharmacies that Benue State actually needs staffed — close to the communities they'll serve.

Scale

Small classes, direct access

As a young college, CCHST offers small class sizes and direct access to staff and leadership rather than the anonymity of a large institution.

What guides us

What we're building toward

A short set of commitments that shape how CCHST teaches and admits students.

Practical over theoretical

Training is oriented toward the day-to-day work of primary health centres, labs, and pharmacies — not exam-only theory.

Community-grounded

Programs are designed around what Benue State's health system needs staffed, from CHEW posts to diagnostic labs.

Accessible entry

Admission is open to SSCE holders (WAEC/NECO), including students still awaiting their result.

Reachable leadership

As a smaller college, students and applicants can reach the Proprietor, Provost, and academic staff directly.
