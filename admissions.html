What you'll need

Typical requirements

Confirm the exact list with the admissions office — this reflects the standard documents most applicants are asked for.

  • Completed application form (₦10,000, available at Cornerstone Academy)
  • SSCE result — WAEC or NECO — or a pending statement of result
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Valid means of identification
