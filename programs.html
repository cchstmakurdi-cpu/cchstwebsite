Our Programs

Four 3-year programmes, each open to SSCE holders. Applications for the 2026/2027 session are open now.

Community Health Extension Worker

CHEW

3 years · SSCE entry

Front-line primary healthcare: immunisation, maternal and child health, health education, and basic treatment for common illnesses in community settings.

  • Primary healthcare principles and community health practice
  • Maternal, newborn, and child health
  • Immunisation and disease prevention
  • Health education and community outreach
Pharmacy Technician

Pharmacy Technician

3 years · SSCE entry

Dispensing, dosage calculation, drug storage, and pharmacy-store management — trained for community pharmacies, hospital pharmacy units, and patent medicine stores.

  • Pharmaceutical dispensing and dosage calculation
  • Drug storage, handling, and inventory management
  • Pharmacy-store operations and record keeping
  • Patient-facing dispensing practice
Medical Laboratory Technician

Medical Laboratory Technician

3 years · SSCE entry

Sample collection, haematology, microbiology, and basic chemical pathology — the technical backbone of every diagnostic lab and hospital pathology unit.

  • Specimen collection and handling
  • Haematology and basic chemical pathology
  • Microbiology and diagnostic testing
  • Laboratory safety and quality control
Public Health Technician

Public Health Technician

3 years · SSCE entry

Disease surveillance, sanitation, health promotion, and outbreak response — trained to work alongside local government health teams on the ground.

  • Disease surveillance and outbreak response
  • Environmental sanitation and hygiene
  • Health promotion and community education
  • Coordination with local government health teams
