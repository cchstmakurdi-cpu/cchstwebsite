Our mission

Since 2023, trained for Benue State's realities.

CCHST trains health workers for the realities of Benue State's health system — not just the theory of a textbook. Read more about us →

Curriculum

Four focused programs

CHEW, Pharmacy Technician, Medical Laboratory Technician, and Public Health Technician — each a 3-year programme open to SSCE holders.

Location

Built in Makurdi, for Benue

Based at Cornerstone Academy on Gboko Road, CCHST trains students for the primary health centres, labs, and pharmacies that Benue State actually needs staffed.

Growth

A young, growing college

Founded in 2023, CCHST is still building — which means small classes, direct access to staff, and a college shaping its identity with its earliest students.

Programs

Four programs, one goal: trained health workers.

Every programme runs 3 years and admits on SSCE results. Applications for the 2026/2027 session are open now.

Community Health Extension Worker

Front-line primary healthcare: immunisation, maternal and child health, health education, and basic treatment for common illnesses in community settings.

Pharmacy Technician

Dispensing, dosage calculation, drug storage, and pharmacy-store management — trained for community pharmacies, hospital pharmacy units, and patent medicine stores.

Medical Laboratory Technician

Sample collection, haematology, microbiology, and basic chemical pathology — the technical backbone of every diagnostic lab and hospital pathology unit.
